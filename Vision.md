# Trail Finder — Vision

## Mission
Become the easiest place in the world for hikers to discover where they should trek.

## Problems We Solve
1. Hikers don't know which trails are currently open.
2. Hikers don't know the best season for each trail.
3. Trail information is scattered across many websites.
4. Planning a trek takes too long.

## Target Users
- **Primary (v1 focus):** Beginner and casual hikers who want simple, trustworthy answers fast.
- **Secondary (design for later):** Experienced hikers who want more detail — but the data structure should support them from day one so we don't have to rebuild.

## Launch Region
Australia and New Zealand. We stay here until trail data (status + season) is accurate and reliable before expanding.

## Product Philosophy
Simple. Fast. Visual. Trustworthy. Beginner-friendly.
Every feature must reduce the time it takes to plan a trek. If it doesn't, we cut it.

## Out of Scope for Now
- Trail booking/permits
- User-generated trip reports or social features
- Regions outside Australia/New Zealand
- Native mobile apps
